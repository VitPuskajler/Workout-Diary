Quick overview


Training Session:
Log your sets as you train, following the plan you already built - weight, reps and RPE for each exercise.

Example:
Upper Body Day: Bench Press - 3 sets of 10 reps @ 70kg (RPE 8), Barbell Row - 3 sets of 10 reps @ 60kg

Workout Plan:
Every exercise and mesocycle you've logged, in one place - and the starting point for building your next one

Example:
8-week plan: Weeks 1-4 Upper/Lower split, Weeks 5-8 Push/Pull/Legs, tracked as one mesocycle

.

Create New Mesocycle Plan:
Start a new training block - name it, set its length, and fill in each day's exercises

Example:
"6-Week Hypertrophy Block" - 4 training days a week, 3-4 sets of 8-12 reps per exercise

Profile:
Change your password, review pending sign-ups, and jump to any part of the app from here

Example:
Deload week: editing Bench Press from 4 sets of 12 reps down to 2 sets of 10 reps

What you need to know

RPE:
Rating of Perceived Exertion - how hard a set actually felt

Example:
RPE 8 on Squats means you had about 2 more reps left in the tank

Sets:
How many rounds of an exercise you plan to do

Example:
"3 sets" of Deadlift means three separate rounds at your chosen rep count

Reps:
How many repetitions you completed in one set

Example:
"10 reps" of Leg Press means ten controlled repetitions in that set

Pauses:
How long you rest between sets

Example:
A 90-second pause after Bench Press lets you recover for a strong next set

Notes:
Anything worth remembering about that exercise or session

Example:
"Left shoulder felt tight - dropped weight on the last set"

Units:
Tap the Kg/Lbs/Other button next to the weight field to change what a set is logged in

Example:
Tap it once to switch from Kg to Lbs - it remembers your choice per exercise next time

About

You can manage all your workouts in one place. After the end of your mesocycle, you can view statistics (currently 'Workout Plan' tab) about your progress and based on it plan a new training period. Aim of this web app is to help you periodize, structure and manage your workouts.

Best Features

  • Guided Training Session
    Follow your mesocycle plan with History and one-tap Repeat, so you're not retyping last time's numbers.
  • Freestyle Training
    Log any exercise on the fly, jump between today's exercises with arrows, and see your best set from last time inline.
  • Editable Progress + Graphs
    Track every lift over time with charts, and edit past sets directly from the same page.
